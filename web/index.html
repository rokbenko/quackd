    Why two controls?

    This is the whole interface the robot has: a twist — three numbers, vx, vy, wz — and a head angle. Two learned policies use them: one stands it up, one walks it. The kick is quackd's own scripted impulse, not a policy. None of it reads English.

    There is no key for say, and no key for walk to the ball and quack. A key carries a command. A sentence needs something to read it — that is the sentence box under Command the duck. A verb leaves a line in the log; a twist leaves a number in the readout.

    These are for a laptop keyboard. With a touchscreen, use the sentence box under Command the duck.
