# Draft: the demo video

What the rules ask of it, then a shot list that meets each ask.

* Under three minutes: aim for 2:45.
* Public on YouTube, its link in the form, in English.
* It shows the project working, and how Token Factory and an NVIDIA model are used.
* Physical AI: at least one continuous minute of the robot hardware operating or, with no
  hardware, of the key application modules in action. This entry has no hardware component, so
  the minute is both acts, unbroken.
* It shows the project on the device it was built for: a computer, and the job on Nebius.
* No third-party trademarks or logos, and no music, unless there is permission for it.

| Time | What is on screen |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:15 | One sentence: robots from different makers meet and cannot say who they are |
| 0:15 to 0:30 | `passport-nebius models` listing the NVIDIA ids on Token Factory |
| 0:30 to 1:45 | The unbroken minute: act one, then act two, two terminals side by side, the simulator views beside them |
| 1:45 to 2:00 | The brake: `doorstep pause`, and the home robot holding still |
| 2:00 to 2:20 | `explain` on the courier's passport: the tier first, then Nemotron's answer, then its own words |
| 2:20 to 2:35 | The Serverless Job and its log on Nebius |
| 2:35 to 2:45 | The repository folder and where to start |

In act one, point at the line in the home robot's log saying a stranger spoke and its words
were kept from the model. In act two, at the courier's words arriving and the two payloads
being compared. If a speed-up is needed in the unbroken minute, say so on screen.

With the SO-101 to hand, `whois --camera` pointed at the real arm puts real hardware on camera
for a few seconds; it does not replace the minute above.
